Background

From environmental
observations to
informed decisions.

I am a PhD student at the College of Natural Resources and Environment, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, and a geospatial researcher working across remote sensing, GIS, climate risk, and spatial decision-making.

My work at the Institute of Remote Sensing and GIS, Jahangirnagar University, has brought together research, teaching, technical training, geospatial databases, and student research support. I have also contributed to development-focused geospatial analysis as a GIS Consultant with the World Bank.

My interests connect satellite and UAV observations with environmental modelling and local knowledge, with a particular focus on Bangladesh.
